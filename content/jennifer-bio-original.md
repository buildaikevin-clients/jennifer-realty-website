Meet Jennifer Barragan

For Jennifer Barragan, real estate isn't simply a career—it's something she grew
up around and has been part of nearly her entire adult life.

Licensed since 2006, Jennifer began her real estate career in Georgia before
later becoming licensed in Alabama and, ultimately, Florida. Over the past two
decades, she has built experience across residential sales, property management,
investing, new construction, fix-and-flips, long-term rentals, and relocation.
After stepping away briefly to focus on her growing family, Jennifer returned to
real estate in Florida and now serves Lakewood Ranch, Sarasota, Bradenton, and
surrounding communities.

Today, Jennifer is proud to call Lakewood Ranch home. As both a resident and real
estate professional, she brings clients firsthand knowledge of the community, its
neighborhoods, lifestyle, amenities, new construction opportunities, and the
nuances that can make one property—or even one street—a better fit than another.

Real estate also runs in Jennifer's family. Her mother, father, and brothers have
all worked as real estate agents, while her father's construction business
exposed her early on to renovation, construction, and the potential of
transforming properties. She was also influenced by an aunt who transitioned from
banking into real estate investing, helping shape Jennifer's understanding of
real estate not only as a place to call home, but as a powerful tool for building
long-term wealth.

That perspective became personal as Jennifer began investing herself. She has
experience with both fix-and-flip projects and long-term rental properties and
continues to own and manage investment properties in Alabama while expanding her
investment interests in Florida. This firsthand experience allows her to evaluate
opportunities through more than just a sales lens—considering value, potential,
marketability, and the long-term implications of a real estate decision.

Whether she is guiding a first-time homebuyer, helping a family relocate,
representing a seller, navigating new construction, or working with an
experienced investor, Jennifer believes her role is to educate first. Her
approach is centered on helping clients understand their options, identify value,
negotiate strategically, and make informed decisions that align with both their
immediate needs and long-term goals.

For Jennifer, a successful real estate transaction isn't simply about getting to
the closing table. It's about helping clients make decisions that can protect,
grow, and ultimately transfer wealth from one generation to the next.

A bilingual English and Spanish speaker, Jennifer is married and a mother of two.
Her life and business are grounded in faith, family, integrity, and a commitment
to serving her clients with the same care and perspective she would want for her
own family.
