# Review Requests. Jennifer Barragan

Ported from kevin-vaz-realty-website/seo/review-requests.md. Reviews are the
highest value, lowest effort item in the whole SEO folder: they move the map
pack, they move what AI chats answer, and they need no website at all. The
Google Business Profile must exist first (see google-business-profile-setup.md).

Target: 10 to 15 Google reviews in the first two months. Zero exist today.

## Jennifer's unfair advantage: twenty years of past clients

A Google review attaches to HER, not to a listing or a state. Clients from
her Georgia and Alabama years count fully, and a 2010 closing remembered
fondly in a 2026 review reads as exactly what it is: a career. Backfill is
the fastest path to the first ten reviews. Make the list before asking
anyone new.

She also works in Spanish. A handful of Spanish language reviews is a direct
signal for "realtor who speaks Spanish in Bradenton", a query with almost no
competition. Ask Spanish speaking clients in Spanish.

## The ask, by text (English)

> Hi [name], it meant a lot working with you on [the house / your sale /
> your move]. I am building my Google presence here on the Gulf Coast and a
> short review from you would genuinely help people find me. It takes about
> a minute: [review link]. Thank you either way!

## The ask, by text (Spanish)

> Hola [name], fue un gusto ayudarte con [la casa / la venta / tu mudanza].
> Estoy construyendo mi presencia en Google aqui en Florida y una resena
> corta tuya me ayudaria muchisimo. Toma como un minuto: [review link].
> Mil gracias de todos modos!

## The follow up. Once, on day 5, never again.

> Hi [name], just floating this back up in case it got buried. One minute,
> and it truly helps: [review link]. Either way, thank you!

Send once. Never nag. A client asked twice was asked once too often.

## Cadence

1. At every closing, in person: "I am going to text you a review link
   tonight, would you mind?" Asked face to face, most people say yes.
2. The text that evening, while the feeling is warm.
3. One follow up on day 5 if nothing landed.
4. Backfill: two or three past clients per week, personally, not as a blast.
5. Reply to EVERY review within a day or two, by name, specifically.

## Compliance lines. Do not cross them.

- Nothing of value in exchange for a review. No gift cards, no raffle
  entries, no discounts. Google removes them and it violates policy.
- No review gating: never filter for happy clients first and only send the
  link to them via a survey tool. Ask everyone.
- Never write or edit a review for a client. Words must be theirs.
- The review link goes to Google. Zillow reviews are also worth asking for
  later, separately, once Google is seeded.
