# Neighborhood hero photographs — source and license

Every image in this folder is a freely licensed photograph from Wikimedia
Commons, resized and cropped to 1800x1013 WebP. Nothing here is stock
photography under a paid license, and nothing here is AI generated.

**The credit line in the bottom left corner of each hero is a license
requirement, not decoration.** CC BY and CC BY-SA both oblige the site to name
the author wherever the work appears. If an image is swapped, its credit must
be swapped with it in the same commit. The credit text lives in `PHOTOS` at the
top of `scripts/build-neighborhoods.js` and is rendered by `areaPage()`.

Cropping and recompressing is permitted under all of these licenses. CC BY-SA
requires that *adaptations* be shared alike; a resized copy used as a page
background does not make the website itself an adaptation, but it is the reason
each file links back to the original so anyone can retrieve the source work.

## Where each image was taken

Sixteen images are of the neighborhood, or of a landmark inside it. **Four are
not** — those areas are recent master planned communities with no freely
licensed photograph in existence. They carry a representative Florida image
instead, their credit line names the real location so no page implies the
picture was taken there, and their alt text is written generically. Those four
are also the ones excluded from the `image` property in the page's Place
schema, because that property asserts the photo depicts the place.

Replacing the four representative images with Jennifer's own photography is the
highest value upgrade available in this folder. Her own photos would also be
license clean and could drop the credit line entirely.

## Regenerating

The sources are downloaded, cropped to the hero ratio and compressed to sit
near 100 KB each. Sizes range from 51 KB to 235 KB; the largest are the grainy
foliage frames, which cost more bits per pixel no matter the quality setting.

Every file is committed, because Netlify runs no build step for this site and
the generated output is the deployed asset.
