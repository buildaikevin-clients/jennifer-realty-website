Ways To Work With Jennifer

Three conversations she has every week.

One of them is probably yours. They all start the same way, with her telling you what the listing photos and the tax record leave out.

Buying

Two houses on the same street can sit four feet apart in elevation and pay very different insurance. That is the sort of thing she checks before you write an offer, along with roof age, what the association holds in reserve, and a real quote on that address.

For Buyers

Selling

Anyone will give you a big number to win the listing. She prices off what has actually closed nearby, not what is still sitting unsold, hands you a net sheet, and tells you how long it takes at that number. If she thinks you should wait, she says so.

Areas Covered

“My job is to educate first. Everything else follows from that.”

Over two decades she has worked residential sales, property management, investing, new construction, fix and flips, long term rentals and relocation. That range is the point. A buyer moving from out of state, a seller trying to price honestly, and an investor looking at a rental are three different conversations, and she has had all three from both sides of the table.

Real estate runs in her family. Her mother, father and brothers have all worked as agents, and her father ran a construction business, so she grew up around renovation and around what it costs to fix what is actually wrong with a house. An aunt who moved from banking into investing taught her to see property as a way to build wealth rather than only as a place to live.

She invests herself. She has done fix and flips and long term rentals, and she still owns and manages property in Alabama while expanding in Florida. It means she reads a listing for value, potential and marketability rather than only for the sale, and she will tell you when the numbers do not work.

For Jennifer a good transaction is not the closing table. It is a decision that holds up years later, protects what you have built, and can pass to the next generation. She lives in Lakewood Ranch with her husband and their two children, and works in English and Spanish.
